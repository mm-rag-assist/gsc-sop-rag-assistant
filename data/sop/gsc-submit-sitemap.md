# GSC - Submit sitemap

This document is intended to provide a **Standard Operating Procedure** for submitting sitemaps in Google Search Console.

## How to submit sitemap in GSC?

1. If the request is not assigned yet, assign it to yourself in the TicketDesk platform by clicking the ***Assign to Me*** button.
2. Start working on the request by clicking the ***Begin Work*** button.
3. Set the **Component** as **SEO / Webmaster Tools**, then click **Save**.
4. In **Case Detail** check the ticket’s **Details**, where the requestor should provide short description and sitemap url to submit.
5. Log in to Google Search Console <https://search.google.com/search-console/about> with searchsupport@democorp.example credentials.
6. Find domain property on the list on the left-hand side. Copy/paste it or start typing its name.
7. From the menu choose **Sitemap** and fill in the **Add a new sitemap** form with provided sitemap url, then click **SUBMIT**.
8. Added sitemap will be visible on the list of submitted sitemaps.
9. If the requestor needs a screenshot, you can send him/her such screenshot.
10. Report the indexing in *DemoCorp WMT Access/Verification Requests Management* file in **Other** tab.
11. Fill in (screenshots omitted in this version):
    - Status (YTS=yet to start, WIP=work in progress, CLD=completed, SUSP=suspended)
    - Search engine: Google
    - Type: Submitting sitemaps
    - Requested URL/Website (paste the submitted sitemaps URLs)
    - \# of requests (number of URLs)
    - Close Date
    - Open Date
    - Ticket (paste the number of ticket)
    - Person Responsible (person that was assigned to manage the ticket)
12. Complete the request:
    1. Respond to the requestor in **Work Notes** in **Additional comments (Customer visible)**, for example:

       ```text
       Hello,
       The sitemap [URL] has been submitted in GSC as requested.

       Once this ticket is closed, you'll receive a brief survey about our support. We'd appreciate your feedback.

       Kind regards,
       ```
    2. In **Closure Information** write **Sitemap submitted**.
    3. Choose **Fulfilled** in **Closure Code**.
    4. Click **Complete work**.
    5. The next day, if there are no comments from the requestor, click **Close record**.

## How to remove sitemap in GSC?

1. If the request is not assigned yet, assign it to yourself in the TicketDesk platform by clicking the ***Assign to Me*** button.
2. Start working on the request by clicking the ***Begin Work*** button.
3. Set the **Component** as **SEO / Webmaster Tools**, then click **Save**.
4. In **Case Detail** check the ticket’s **Details**, where the requestor should provide short description and sitemap url to remove.
5. Log in to Google Search Console <https://search.google.com/search-console/about> with searchsupport@democorp.example credentials.
6. Find domain property on the list on the left-hand side. Copy/paste it or start typing its name.
7. From the menu choose **Sitemap** and then click on a sitemap URL you need to remove.
8. From right menu (three dots) select **Remove sitemap**.
9. If the requestor needs a screenshot, you can send him/her such screenshot.
10. Report the indexing in *DemoCorp WMT Access/Verification Requests Management* file in **Other** tab.
11. Fill in (screenshots omitted in this version):
    - Status (YTS=yet to start, WIP=work in progress, CLD=completed, SUSP=suspended)
    - Search engine: Google
    - Type: Removing sitemaps
    - Requested URL/Website (paste the submitted sitemaps URLs)
    - \# of requests (number of URLs)
    - Close Date
    - Open Date
    - Ticket (paste the number of ticket)
    - Person Responsible (person that was assigned to manage the ticket)
12. Complete the request:
    1. Respond to the requestor in **Work Notes** in **Additional comments (Customer visible)**, for example:

       ```text
       Hello,
       The sitemap [sitemap URL] has been removed in GSC as requested.

       Once this ticket is closed, you'll receive a brief survey about our support. We'd appreciate your feedback.

       Kind regards,
       ```
    2. In **Closure Information** write **Sitemap removed**.
    3. Choose **Fulfilled** in **Closure Code**.
    4. Click **Complete work**.
    5. The next day, if there are no comments from the requestor, click **Close record**.

## How to resubmit sitemap in GSC?

In order to resubmit a sitemap, follow the steps [How to submit sitemap in GSC](#how-to-submit-sitemap-in-gsc).

## What if the requestor has attached a sitemap file instead of a url?

1. Respond to the requestor in **Work Notes** in **Additional comments (Customer visible)**:

   ```text
   To submit sitemap in GSC you must provide us sitemap url - not a file.

   Please submit a new ticket once the sitemap will be uploaded to the site.

   I'am closing the ticket now.
   ```
2. Close the ticket:
   1. In **Closure Information** write **Request rejected**.
   2. Choose **Request Rejected** in **Closure Code**.
   3. Click **Complete work**.
3. Then click **Close Record**.
