# GSC - Update or Remove website

This document is intended to provide a **Standard Operating Procedure** for removing and updating websites in Google Search Console.

## How to remove or update website in GSC?

1. If the request is not assigned yet, assign it to yourself in the TicketDesk platform by clicking the ***Assign to Me*** button.
2. Start working on the request by clicking the ***Begin Work*** button.
3. Set the **Component** as **SEO / Webmaster Tools**, then click **Save**.
4. In **Case Detail** check the ticket’s **Details**, where the requestor should provide short description of the task (removal or update).
5. Log in to Google Search Console <https://search.google.com/search-console/about> with searchsupport@democorp.example credentials.
6. Find domain property on the list on the left-hand side. Copy/paste it or start typing its name.
7. To remove domain property go to step 9.
8. To update property go to step 14.
9. To remove domain property go to **Settings** and use **Remove property** button in **About** section.
10. A confirmation window will appear and there again click on the **Remove property** button.
11. Report domain property removal in *DemoCorp WMT Access/Verification Requests Management* file in ***Other*** tab.
12. Fill in (screenshots omitted in this version):
    - Status (YTS=yet to start, WIP=work in progress, CLD=completed, SUSP=suspended)
    - Search engine: Google
    - Type: Removing domain
    - Requested URL/Website (paste the submitted sitemaps URLs)
    - \# of requests (number of URLs)
    - Close Date
    - Open Date
    - Ticket (paste the number of ticket)
    - Person Responsible (person that was assigned to manage the ticket)
13. Go to step 19 to complete the request.
14. To update domain property go to **Settings** and click **Change of address** in **General settings** section.
15. From drop down menu select relevant domain property and click **Validate & Update** button.
16. After validation the update status will be shown. You can make screenshot and attach in the ticket.
17. Report domain url update in *DemoCorp WMT Access/Verification Requests Management* file in ***Other*** tab.
18. Fill in (screenshots omitted in this version):
    - Status (YTS=yet to start, WIP=work in progress, CLD=completed, SUSP=suspended)
    - Search engine: Google
    - Type: Change of address
    - Requested URL/Website (paste the submitted sitemaps URLs)
    - \# of requests (number of URLs)
    - Close Date
    - Open Date
    - Ticket (paste the number of ticket)
    - Person Responsible (person that was assigned to manage the ticket)
19. Complete the request:
    1. Respond to the requestor in **Work Notes** in **Additional comments (Customer visible)**. Use the matching reply template below.
    2. In **Closure Information** write **Domain removed from GSC** or **Domain updated in GSC**.
    3. Choose **Fulfilled** in **Closure Code**.
    4. Click **Complete work**.
    5. The next day, if there are no comments from the requestor, click **Close record**.
